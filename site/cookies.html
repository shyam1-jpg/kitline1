Cookie policy

This policy explains cookies used on kiteline.uk and the Kiteline app.

What are cookies?

Small text files stored on your device to remember preferences, keep you signed in, or understand how the site is used.

Essential cookies

  • Session / auth — keep you logged in to the app and Academy (HttpOnly where configured).
  • Security — CSRF and CAPTCHA tokens where enabled.

Functional cookies

Language preference on Academy, UI settings, and similar non-essential preferences stored locally or in cookies.

Analytics

We do not use third-party advertising cookies on the marketing site today. If we add privacy-friendly analytics, this page will be updated.

Third parties

Stripe checkout and Cloudflare Turnstile may set cookies when those features are enabled. See their policies for details.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser; essential features such as login may not work without them.
