Pricing FAQs

Can I pay on this website?

Yes — pick your team size and click Subscribe to pay with Stripe (card). Try the app free first. If checkout is not live yet, email us for an invoice.

What if I need more users later?

Upgrade to the next tier in Settings or on this pricing page. Your plan limits how many people you can add on Team — e.g. the 5-user plan allows up to 5 staff accounts.

Do you discount large teams?

Yes — the 50-user tier is priced lower per person than smaller plans. For 50+ users or multiple hotels, email us for an enterprise quote.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — every new account gets 14 days free with full access for up to 5 users. No card needed to register. After 14 days, pick a paid plan to continue.

Do you sell temperature sensors now?

Not as a standard product yet. The app supports manual logging and simulated readings today. We are running a small hardware pilot to choose reliable probes and gateways before we publish fixed sensor pricing.

Are the prices final?

No. Figures on this page are guide prices for early access and may change before public launch. Your signed quote is the price that applies to you.

Is support included?

Yes — email support for early-access customers during UK business hours. Response times improve as we grow the team.

Prices shown are early-access guides. Subscriptions billed monthly via Stripe until cancelled.
