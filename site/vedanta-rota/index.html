💾 Data & Backup

Cloud (primary): Firebase Firestore project the-vedanta — staff, rotas, clock times, leave, PINs & login activity sync from every phone, tablet and PC.

Local: Each device also keeps a copy in browser storage (works offline, syncs when back online).

Email backup: Automatic reports sent to Operation@thevedanta.org

  • Weekly — every Monday ~7:00 (UK)
  • Monthly — 27th of each month ~7:00 (UK)

🔐 Department PINs

Each department manager uses their PIN to log in and access only their rota. The Admin PIN gives full access to all departments, staff management, and PIN settings.

⚠️ PINs are stored on this device only. Share new PINs with managers in person.
